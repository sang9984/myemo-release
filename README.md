# myemo

A tiny companion that lives in your Mac's notch and reacts to your hand gestures,
typing, and cursor with dozens of playful expressions. **100% on-device — no
network access, no data collected.**

## Features

- Lives in the notch — a compact character at the top-center of your screen.
- Reacts to hand gestures (thumbs up, heart, peace sign, and more) via the camera.
- Reacts to typing and follows your cursor with its eyes.
- Dozens of moods and animations.
- Menu-bar controls (show/hide Dock icon, info, quit).

## Privacy

myemo processes everything locally with Apple's Vision framework. The camera is
used only to recognize hand gestures on-device; no image or video is ever stored
or transmitted, and the app has no network access. See
[PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details.
