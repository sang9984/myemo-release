# Privacy Policy — myemo

_Last updated: June 7, 2026_

myemo ("the app") is a macOS desktop companion that displays a small character
near your Mac's notch and reacts to hand gestures, typing activity, and cursor
movement. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

**myemo collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere.**
The app has no network access of any kind.

## Camera

If you grant camera permission, myemo uses the camera **only** to recognize hand
gestures and basic face presence locally, using Apple's on-device Vision
framework.

- Camera frames are processed in real time on your Mac and immediately discarded.
- No image, video, or still frame is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted.
- Camera access is entirely optional. Without it, myemo still reacts to typing
  and cursor movement.

You can revoke camera access at any time in
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera**.

## Typing

myemo detects *that* typing is occurring by reading the system's aggregate
key-press counter. It does **not** read, log, or have any way to determine which
keys are pressed. It is not a keylogger and requires no Input Monitoring
permission.

## Cursor

When mouse tracking is enabled, the character's eyes follow your pointer. The
cursor position is used only to animate the character on screen and is never
stored or transmitted.

## Data Storage

The only data myemo stores is your local app preferences (e.g. whether the Dock
icon, camera, and mouse tracking are enabled), saved on your Mac via macOS
UserDefaults. This never leaves your device.

## Data Sharing

myemo does not share any data with the developer or any third party, because it
collects none and has no network capability.

## Children's Privacy

myemo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
